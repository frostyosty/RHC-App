# The cast

**Any time.** Read this when writing conversations (07), tuning the engine (06),
planning visits (08), or drawing people ([art](art.md)).

Rachel and Alfred come from the user (2026-09-27), and so do two facts about them:
Rachel loves paintings, and the two of them don't get on. Everything else here is a
**proposal** (Ask). The user decides the names, who the characters are, and how they
fit together.

## The setting

Your street in a Tauranga suburb: weatherboard houses, a feijoa tree over the fence,
the harbour on a clear day, the local church, the library and the community garden.
The characters are neighbours and people from round about. NZ English throughout.

Māori and Pasifika characters are written with care: te reo and customs are used
correctly and naturally, there are no stereotypes, and the user reviews their lines.

## How to read a sheet

- **Traits** are 06's seven: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion,
  agreeableness, neuroticism, pride and discretion, each from −1 to 1.
- **Loves** and **dislikes** are tastes (from 06's tag list), strongest first.
- **Cares about** gives the weights of their standards.
- **Story** is their concerns in order, and what moves each one on.
- **Rhythm** is when they tend to visit (08).
- **Bonds** are their starting relationships (affinity, and respect where it
  matters), and why.
- **Voice** is how they talk, with example lines.
