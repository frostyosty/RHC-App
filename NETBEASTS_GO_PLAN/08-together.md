# 08. Together outdoors

**Needs:** 04; `NETBEASTS_PLAN.md` §4 (multiplayer) and phase 5 (the red-wall
colossus) · **Size:** L
**Read first:** [README](README.md), rules 5 and 6; `NETBEASTS_PLAN.md` §4 and
phase 5; [04](04-beasts-at-real-places.md) → Interfaces.

## This is a sketch

It isn't designed yet. It can't be until multiplayer exists in the Wilds,
because it stands on whatever that turns out to be: who runs the sim, how
phones find each other, and what happens when two players are at one beast.
Those are Asks in `NETBEASTS_PLAN.md` §4, and their answers apply here. Come
back and write this step properly when §4 has landed.

## What it would give

- **Seeing each other.** Players nearby who've switched it on show on the
  radar. Rule 6 already means they see the same beasts.
- **One fight, several players.** Two players in reach of the same beast are
  in the same fight, each seeing it from where they stand. Step 06 drew the
  fight as a view of the sim, so each phone is just another view.
- **The colossus, outdoors.** Phase 5 brings the red-wall invader into the
  Wilds. Outdoors it could be something to gather for: it stands at a place
  for a slot, and it takes more than one player to bring it down.

## What makes it hard

- **Where you are would leave the phone.** Everything before this step keeps
  it there (rule 5). Seeing each other means sending it somewhere. Keep it
  coarse (the cell, never the point), only while the radar is open, and only
  for players who've switched it on.
- **Strangers.** Showing where a player is to anyone nearby is a real risk,
  more so for a young player. Friends only is the safer shape.
- **Agreeing on what's there.** With step 07's streets, two phones can differ
  on a spawn. The host's list wins.

## Ask (when this step is written properly)

- Seeing other players at all, and whether that's friends only. Default: off
  unless switched on, friends only, never finer than a cell.
- The colossus outdoors: for everybody in the area, or only the player whose
  wall dropped, with friends invited?
